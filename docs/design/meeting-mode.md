# Shape: Meeting mode (M5 take 1)

**The job.** Keenan at a kitchen table at dusk, iPad landscape, homeowner beside him. He talks; the screen shows. Three jobs in one surface: present Vesper, make it about this house, price it live. Nothing to read, nothing internal, nothing that smells like software.

**Direction.** A deck, not an app. Full-bleed navy screens, one idea each, swiped like photos. Type does the talking: a short line large, a supporting line small. Gold only where light or action is: the one accent per screen, the total, the held moment. Photos full-bleed with text over a quiet scrim. The quote screen is the only working screen, and even it reads like a bill laid on the table: lines on the left, one big number on the right.

**The screens, in order.**
1. Cover: the wordmark, the customer's street name small. (Derived, not editable; sets the tone.)
2. What Vesper is: title + 2 short lines.
3. Hidden by day: day photo with a drag handle revealing night. Caption line.
4. The whites first: title + 2 lines (photo optional later).
5. How install day works: 3 to 4 short numbered lines.
6. The warranty: the exact wording, as a calm list.
7. Gallery: full-bleed photos, swiped within the page.
8. This house, the mockup: their house lit, full screen, pinch to zoom.
9. This house, the colour: soffit answer, matched track colour by name, initials field + confirm. The one form moment before the quote; treat it like signing a card, not filling a form.
10. The quote: builder left (runs, boxes, adders, discounts, colour timing, financing, valid until), the bill right (lines, total, "+ GST" only when GST is on). Save quietly states saved or no-signal.

**States.** Any content page without its content is skipped entirely. Colour not set: the colour screen says "to be confirmed with Keenan" and the initials wait. No mockup: screen 8 skipped. Offline: everything above works; Save and Confirm say "No signal, nothing lost" plainly.

**Navigation.** Horizontal snap swiping; small dots bottom centre; chevrons at the edges for thumb taps; a small ghost X top left to leave (back to where he came from). No tab bar, no header, no theme toggle. Progress is spatial, not numbered.

**Constraints.** Navy #132E3F surfaces, gold #B99257 accents only; Manrope, big; no gradients, glass, emoji or animation beyond the swipe and the slider. Customer-facing copy plain, no em dashes, no claims we can't back. Tap targets 44 px for Keenan's controls. Works at 1194x834 first; tolerable on a phone.

**Out of scope for take 1.** The folder PDF, accept/present flows, deep motion, per-page custom layouts in Settings.
