# Assessment A: Design Review, meeting mode (take 1, before polish)
*Evidence: all 16 named captures (10 iPad deck pages, owner lead card, 3 Settings editors, 2 phone), the shape doc (docs/design/meeting-mode.md), PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md, and source: meeting-mode.tsx, quote-screen.tsx, day-night-slider.tsx, pinch-zoom.tsx, mockup-card.tsx, meeting/[leadId]/page.tsx, meeting/actions.ts, meeting-card.tsx, presentation-images.tsx, lib/pricing.ts, lib/presentation.ts, lib/time.ts, public/sw.js, and migration 20261102075917.*

## 1. Design specificity verdict

A real share of this deck could only belong to Vesper's dusk meeting. It is forced dark regardless of device theme because the customer is watching (`dark fixed inset-0`, meeting-mode.tsx:203); the cover carries the customer's own street; the quote builder speaks Calgary roofline ("Front upper", "Left gable", "Garage"); the bill reads like a paper bill laid on the table, lines left, one big gold number, "No payment until your install is complete"; the colour page is treated like signing a card, with the initials stored to the lead and the timeline; GST is correctly silent while disabled; there are no swatches anywhere; and the whole thing survives a dead spot via localStorage drafts and plain no-signal copy. The warranty wording is Keenan's own, verbatim from settings (roadmap line 151 confirms it was given, not invented). This is a surface shaped by its spec, not a template.

What is still generic scaffolding: the builder column is admin-form texture dropped onto a customer-facing page, stock inputs, a native locale-formatted date control, a datalist, a "Lower/Upper" toggle button, and an admin hint sentence at customer eye level. The five text pages are interchangeable title-plus-paragraph slides whose headline rides up and down with content length, the pager dots are stock carousel furniture at sub-44px size, and the caption plate over photos is a one-size box that leaves an empty dark slab hanging over the day photo. The deck half is Vesper; the working half still smells like software, which the shape doc explicitly forbade.

## 3. Cognitive load

The customer holds almost nothing, which is the deck's best quality: one idea per page, the price assembled line by line as they watch, the total in one number, no GST arithmetic while it is off. The two moments they must act (initials, watching the price move) are single-step.

Keenan carries the load. He must remember which pages obey a swipe and which quietly refuse it (3, 8, and the gallery's nested scroller); that tapping "Lower" toggles rather than opens; that the murky olive Confirm means disabled-until-typed; that nothing will tell him the quote on screen is unsaved or that a week-old local draft may be shadowing the server copy; and that Save needs signal while everything else does not. The builder also makes him do rate arithmetic (Per foot, Per box fields) at customer eye level, where a slip is public. Each is small; together they are exactly the "smells like software" burden the shape doc banned from this table.

## 4. What must change before tomorrow's meeting (P0) and what should (P1)

**P0**

1. **"try again before they leave" shows to the homeowner.** The colour screen's failure copy (meeting-mode.tsx:412 and actions.ts:189) refers to the customers in the third person on the screen they are reading, right after they initialled. It exposes the close mechanics at the exact trust moment. Smallest fix: reword both strings to "No signal. The initials were not saved. Try again in a moment." and stop labelling the server's DB-failure path "No signal".
2. **Offline images almost certainly do not cache.** Every deck `<img>` loads a cross-origin signed Supabase URL without `crossOrigin`, so the fetch is no-cors, the response is opaque, `response.ok` is false, and sw.js:52 refuses to cache it (source). The M5 promise, opened once with signal and it keeps working, breaks precisely at the weak-signal kitchen table: a reload mid-meeting shows a navy deck with broken photos and no mockup. Smallest fix: add `crossorigin="anonymous"` to meeting-mode, slider, zoom and gallery images (or accept opaque responses for the IMAGES cache), then verify once in airplane mode.
3. **The phone price page paints the bill over the builder.** phone-02-the-price.png shows "Track and lights", "Your price" and a giant gold $500 colliding with the Discounts controls. Mechanism (source): quote-screen.tsx:195 gives the bill a `1fr` row that collapses to zero under the tall builder, and :425's `min-h-0` + `justify-center` centres the bill on that line, spilling upward over the controls. Meeting mode is iPad-first, but the phone is the stated fallback and this fails "tolerable". Smallest fix: below `lg`, make both rows `auto`, drop `justify-center`, and let the page scroll as one column.
4. **"Prices changed here apply to this quote only." sits at customer eye level.** quote-screen.tsx:282, visible in ipad-10. It is a note to Keenan, printed on the customer's screen, announcing mid-negotiation that the rate is changeable on the spot. Smallest fix: delete the sentence; the behaviour is already documented where it belongs, in Settings.

**P1**

5. **Pages 3 and 8 hijack the deck gesture.** `touch-none` across the whole day-night slider (day-night-slider.tsx:23) and pinch-zoom (pinch-zoom.tsx:33) means the swipe that moves every other page does nothing there, a silent stall mid-presentation. Fix: leave a swipeable gutter at each screen edge, or bind the slider drag to the handle and caption-free zones only.
6. **The caption plate renders as a floating empty box.** On ipad-03 the single `bg-background/75` plate (meeting-mode.tsx:318) is as wide as its longest line, so the short title leaves a tall blank slab that turns visible the moment it crosses onto the day photo, at the default 50% handle it reads as a rendering bug. Fix: give title and body their own fitted plates (`w-fit` per element).
7. **Stale local draft can shadow the server quote, unseen.** `stored?.quote ?? draft` (meeting-mode.tsx:99-104) always prefers localStorage, even when the server draft is newer, and nothing marks the screen as diverged. The one number in the room could be last week's. Fix: prefer whichever of `stored.savedAt` and `draft.updatedAt` is newer, and show "Edited since last save" next to the Save button whenever state differs from the saved draft.
8. **The gallery cannot be reordered though the editor promises order.** Settings says "the gallery shows at the meeting in this order" but presentation-images.tsx offers only add and remove, so reordering means deleting and re-uploading. Fix: move up/down buttons writing through `withPresentationImage`.

**Smaller notes (fold into take 2):** pager dots are 24px targets and near-invisible over the light day photo; text-page headlines jump vertically between pages (swap `justify-center` for a fixed top alignment); the disabled Confirm reads as murky olive rather than clearly inactive; blank adder and discount labels print as "Adder" and "Discount" on the customer bill; the cover's hardcoded subtitle repeats as page 2's seeded title, a stutter on the first swipe; the native date input shows device-locale "11/16/2026" beside the bill's long-form date (the bill's own ISO date seen on the capture was already fixed in commit 502c8e2, nine minutes after capture); the gallery stacks two progress systems ("1 / 3" plus the deck dots); re-opening the deck always starts at the cover with no way to resume at the price; arrow-key paging dies once focus enters any control; the right chevron at 40% opacity nearly vanishes over the day sky; the lead card's copy "so This house has their home in lights" reads as a typo, quote the page name; "Night"/"Day" chips render asymmetrically across the two halves.
