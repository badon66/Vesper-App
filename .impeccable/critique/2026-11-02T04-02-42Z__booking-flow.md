# Assessment A: Design Review, setter booking flow (before redesign)

Surface: setter Home, Schedule, tapping a time, "Book a meeting / Which lead?", "Before you book", and New lead with a held time. Evidence: the phone (390 px) and iPad (1194 px) setter screenshots plus the listed source.

## 1. Design specificity verdict

The words and the main component are written for Vesper; the overall structure could belong to any app. The time tile could only belong to Vesper's evening schedule: 80 px tiles with big tabular times, the state written out ("Open", "Your booking · ...", "[Name] is booking" with the holder's initials), refreshed every 3 seconds. The copy is just as specific ("Nobody else can pick it while you book", "Homeowner said no", "Use where I'm standing"). The flow around the tiles is stock CRM scaffolding built from stock outline buttons and bordered cards: a record picker ("Which lead?"), one long record form (New lead) and a validation page with red-bordered rows and "Edit lead" links. That structure follows the data model (a meeting needs a lead, a lead needs fields), not the door conversation (open, find out, photos, book). Navy and gold are applied like a skin rather than used to point the eye, so gold sits on every open tile, the selected chip, the FAB and the hold card at once.

## 3. Cognitive load

This checklist covers the booking flow as a whole; Home and Schedule on their own would pass most items.

- **Single focus: FAIL.** Before you book stacks a lot on one screen under a live 5-item tab bar whose gold FAB floats over the list:
  - the hold card with two exits;
  - six readiness rows, three of them with links that leave the page;
  - the photo-skip answer and the decision-maker checkbox;
  - "Still needed" and the Book button.
- **Chunking: FAIL.** Before you book is 8 requirements plus extras. New lead's Find out puts 6 questions in one block, and "Where the lead came from" has 5 options.
- **Grouping: FAIL.** The photo-skip question ("Why are photos missing?") is cut off from the photos row by the Map position row. On New lead the booking questions sit above the address, far from Find out and Photos.
- **Visual hierarchy: PARTIAL.** On Home and Schedule the times clearly lead. On Before you book the strongest element is the gold hold card with its exits. The primary action is about two phone screens down and disabled, and Done and Missing rows share one box style.
- **One thing at a time: FAIL.** New lead asks for everything on one page. Before you book asks the setter to fix three different things and answer two more, all at once.
- **Minimal choices: FAIL.** See the decision points below.
- **Working memory: FAIL.** After "Edit lead" the setter has to remember what was missing, because the edit form doesn't show it. On Home the undated booking times mean recalling which day each one is.
- **Progressive disclosure: PASS.** The Door guide starts closed, "Say why" appears only after Other, the photo-skip question appears only with fewer than 3 guided photos, and New lead shows the booking questions only while a time is held.

**Result:** 6 failures and 1 partial, which is high cognitive load (the critical band).

Decision points with more than 4 visible options:
- **Which lead?:** "Add a new lead and book it" plus up to 50 of the setter's New and Follow up leads, newest first, with no search.
- **Before you book (phone):** 9 controls on the page plus 5 tabs. The 9 are Pick another time, Let go, Edit lead (twice), Take photos, Homeowner said no, Other, the checkbox and Book this time.
- **New lead with a time held:** nearly 50 controls in one scroll, including "Where the lead came from" with 5 options.
- **Schedule:** 15 evening chips (Tonight plus 14 days) in a sideways strip, plus 6 times. The times are one choice of a single kind and are fine; the chips give no overview of the two weeks.
- **Tab bar:** 5 items stay live while a time is held on the booking page, and 4 of them let go of the hold without a word.

## 4. Emotional journey (setter at the door)

- **Start, Home:** calm and confident. "Hello, TEST" and six big times the homeowner can read over the setter's shoulder.
- **Peak, tapping a time:** the tile turns "Holding", then a new page says "You're holding Sat, Nov 14 at 5:00 p.m. Nobody else can pick it while you book." This is the best moment in the flow, but three things undercut it:
  - the next sentence is a warning ("It lets go if you leave or close the app");
  - the only buttons on the card are exits (Pick another time, Let go);
  - on the phone, "p.m." drops onto a line of its own.
- **Valley 1, "Which lead?":** a database question at the high point of the visit. For a new homeowner the right answer is an outline button that looks like a list row.
- **Valley 2, New lead:** the hold card is gone after one swipe. For the next five screens nothing says the time is still held, while the homeowner waits through Christmas lights, areas, timing, house type, email and source. If the phone locked in a pocket for 2 minutes, the time is gone, and the card above still says Holding.
- **Valley 3 (deepest), the bounce:** if anything is missing, Save and book lands on a page of red "Missing:" boxes. The tick and the photo reason just given are blank again, and every fix link gives the time away without a word. All of this happens in front of the homeowner.
- **End:** a successful booking ends on one small bordered sentence on the lead page: "Booked for Sat, Nov 14 at 5:00 p.m. Confirm the time with the homeowner before you leave." It is correct but quiet, and nothing is made to show the homeowner. By the peak-end rule the setter will remember the red checklist more than the booking.

## 5. Strengths

1. **The time tile is a real signature component.**
   - 80 px tap targets with large tabular times.
   - State in words as well as colour: "Open", "Your booking · TEST (automated)", "[Name] is booking" with the holder's initials, polled every 3 seconds.
   - Date chips that count ("6 open", "No meetings").
   - It reads at arm's length, which matters when the homeowner is choosing too.
2. **The copy is plain, local and honest.**
   - "Only the address and first name are needed to save."
   - "Answer the soffit colour. Not sure is fine."
   - "Hold the phone sideways and get the whole roofline in."
   - Errors give a next step: "That time was just taken. Here are the open times now."
3. **The guardrails are real and visible.**
   - The database decides every hold, and held or booked times can't be tapped.
   - Book stays off until every requirement passes.
   - New lead's pinned bar puts the gold "Save and book" at the bottom right, with X and "Save lead" beside it, all 44 px or taller.

## 6. Priority issues

**[P1] 1. The fix links on Before you book quietly give the time away.**
- **What:** "Edit lead" (to /app/leads/[id]/edit) and "Take photos" (to /app/leads/[id]) are not booking paths, so the hold keeper lets the hold go the moment either is tapped. `isBookingPath` in lib/schedule/hold-store.ts allows only /app/schedule/book and /app/leads/new, and the release happens in the first effect of components/schedule/hold-keeper.tsx. Every tab except New lead does the same. There is no warning, and saving the edit lands on the lead page, not back in booking.
- **Why it matters:** the setter does exactly what the screen asks, and the time the homeowner just agreed to is free for any other setter. They then have to find "Book a meeting", pick the time again, tick again and answer again. It breaks the banner's own promise, "Nobody else can pick it while you book."
- **Fix:** make every missing item fixable on the booking screen itself:
  - Soffit colour, Owns the home and Wants a price as the same one-tap answer chips;
  - Phone as an inline tel field;
  - Map position as the address field plus "Use where I'm standing";
  - each guided photo as an inline camera button.

  While a time is held, the booking's own bottom bar replaces the tab bar. If leaving is truly needed, confirm first ("Leaving lets 5:00 p.m. go") and return to the same booking with the hold kept.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden, then /impeccable layout for the inline fixes.

**[P1] 2. The flow follows the database, not the door.**
- **What:** time-first booking for a new homeowner goes like this:
  1. Tap a time.
  2. "Book a meeting / Which lead?"
  3. "Add a new lead and book it" (an outline button, not the primary).
  4. New lead: one page about 3,350 px tall on a 390 px phone. After the held-time card come "Everyone who decides..." and the photo-skip reason, both above the address. The other requirements are scattered: Phone is the 5th field, Owns, Wants and Soffit sit among six Find out questions, and the guided photos come after that, followed by house type, notes, email and source.
  5. Save and book, and if anything is missing, a second checklist page.
- **Why it matters:** the homeowner is standing there while the setter scrolls about five screens. Questions come out of the conversation's order: the setter confirms the decision-makers before they have entered the address, and gives a reason for skipped photos before trying the camera. That puts at risk both the 30-second capture goal and honest answers.
- **Fix:** while a time is held, show a short sequence in door order, one decision per screen, with a visible step count:
  1. who and where (address or GPS, first name, phone);
  2. the two qualifying answers plus soffit colour;
  3. the three guided photos or a skip reason;
  4. confirm the decision-makers;
  5. Book.

  Default the time-first path to a new lead, with "A lead I already saved" as a secondary link that has search. Move the optional fields (Christmas lights, areas, timing, house type, notes, email, source) after booking or into a closed "More details".
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable shape, then /impeccable distill.

**[P1] 3. The hold's status can be wrong, and its biggest risk is never stated.**
- **What:**
  - On New lead, the gold "Holding Sat, Nov 14 at 5:00 p.m." card scrolls away with the first swipe, and nothing shows the hold while the rest of the form is filled in.
  - When the hold lapses (phone locked past the 2-minute grace, the camera reloading the app, no signal), the screen still claims it. HoldOnNewLead keeps its own `held` state and HoldBanner keeps its server-rendered `held`, so "Nobody else can pick it" stays on screen. Meanwhile a separate fixed alert says "Your hold on 5:00 p.m. let go. Pick the time again." and offers only "OK".
  - The banner copy, "It lets go if you leave or close the app", leaves out the likeliest cause at a door: the phone locking while the setter talks.
- **Why it matters:** the setter tells the homeowner "you're booked for 5:00" based on a status that may be false. Two messages that contradict each other on one screen wear down trust in the whole hold system.
- **Fix:** add one persistent hold bar, pinned above the bottom action bar on every held screen and bound to the hold store, not page state. It shows the time and "Held for you". When the hold is lost it changes in place to "5:00 p.m. let go", with a gold "Hold it again" if the time is still open. Rewrite the copy to state the rule, reading the grace period from settings: "Held while this screen is on. If your phone locks for 2 minutes, it lets go."
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify, then /impeccable harden.

**[P2] 4. Before you book reads like a failure report, and its main action is out of reach.**
- **What:**
  - Three "Done:" rows use the same bordered boxes as the "Missing:" rows and fill the space above the fold.
  - Missing items are outlined in Warning Red (DESIGN.md: errors only), and the homeowner can see them.
  - "Why are photos missing?" is split from the photos row by the Map position row.
  - "Still needed:" repeats the same list.
  - Labels are written for the done state: "Missing: Soffit colour answered", "Done: Owns the home: Yes".
  - "Book this time" sits at the end of the page, about two phone screens down and not pinned. Disabled, it is a muddy gold-brown with dim text.
  - The only buttons above the fold are the exits.
- **Why it matters:** at the door the setter has to scan nine boxes to find three to-dos, then scroll to find the action. The red reads as "something is wrong with this customer".
- **Fix:**
  - one summary line for what is done ("Phone, owns the home, wants a price: done");
  - each missing item as its own inline control, with the photo-skip reason inside the photo item;
  - neutral or gold styling for to-dos, keeping red for real errors;
  - labels written per state ("Soffit colour: not answered yet");
  - drop "Still needed" or turn it into the button's caption ("2 things left");
  - a pinned bottom bar with "Book Sat 5:00 p.m." at the bottom right, and Pick another time and Let go as small secondary actions in the same bar.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout, then /impeccable clarify.

**[P2] 5. The three ways into booking behave and talk differently, and a bounce loses answers.**
- **What:**
  - The same things get different words: "You're holding" vs "Holding", "Pick another time" vs "Change the time", "Why are photos missing?" vs "If you can't take the Front, Left corner and Right corner photos, why not?", "Book this time" vs "Save and book".
  - Tapping a time on Home or Schedule moves to a new page, while "Hold a time" on New lead opens a picker inline.
  - New lead pins its action bar; Before you book does not.
  - A failed "Save and book" redirects to Before you book with the "Everyone who decides" tick, the photo-skip reason and its note all cleared, because BookingForm starts from `everyone=false, skip=null`. That goes against CLAUDE.md's rule that a form keeps everything typed after an error.
- **Why it matters:** setters have to learn three versions of one task, and answering twice at the door looks sloppy and costs time.
- **Fix:** use one component and one set of words for the held time, the skip reason and the final action across every entry point. Carry the tick and the skip reason through the redirect, or keep them with the hold, so a bounce never asks twice.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable polish for the consistency, /impeccable harden for carrying the answers over.

## 7. Persona red flags

**Jordan (confused first-timer; a setter in week one):**
- Taps a time with a brand-new homeowner and gets "Which lead?". Jordan doesn't know whether this person is a "lead" yet, and the right choice ("Add a new lead and book it") looks like an ordinary row.
- On New lead, the first thing under the hold card is "Everyone who decides on the house will be at the meeting", then "If you can't take the Front, Left corner and Right corner photos, why not?", both before the address. Jordan answers literally and early, probably tapping "Homeowner said no" before trying the camera.
- Can't tell what "Let go" does (cancel? lose the time?), and it has no confirm.
- "Missing: Soffit colour answered" reads as a contradiction. "Edit lead" opens a long form with no pointer, then lands on the lead page with the booking gone and no hint to tap "Book a meeting".
- After booking, Home lists "5:35 p.m." with no day, so Jordan thinks the meeting is tonight.

**Riley (stress tester):**
- Taps "Edit lead" on Before you book and the hold goes at once, without a word. The back button returns to "5:00 p.m. is open, but not held for you yet", or, if someone took it meanwhile, "[Name] is booking this time".
- Locks the phone on New lead for 3 minutes and gets two contradicting messages on one screen: the gold "Holding... Nobody else can pick it" card and the "Your hold on 5:00 p.m. let go" alert.
- Taps Save and book with no phone number and is bounced to Before you book with the tick, the photo reason and the note all cleared.
- Taps the Leads tab mid-booking to look up the neighbour, and the hold is gone with no warning.
- Looks for an older Follow up lead in "Which lead?" and can't reach it: the list stops at the newest 50 and has no search.

**Project persona, a setter at a door at dusk, one-handed** (PRODUCT.md: 4 to 6 pm, phone in one hand, homeowner in the doorway, weak signal, phones that lock, a camera that can reload the app):
- Every booking screen puts its only buttons at the top ("Pick another time", "Let go"), out of thumb reach, while "Book this time" is about two screens down and not pinned.
- In the phone capture of Before you book, the gold New lead FAB floats over the first Missing row, and every tab except New lead is a one-tap way to lose the time.
- Pocketing the phone while talking locks it. After 2 minutes the hold is gone, and nothing told the setter it would be.
- The requirements are spread across a 3,350 px form, so the setter scrolls up and down one-handed: to the decision-maker box at the top, then to the photos near the bottom.
- In bright light and at dusk:
  - the chips' "6 open" is 12 px;
  - the disabled "Book this time" is a muddy brown with dim text and looks broken;
  - the unticked native checkbox renders as a grey filled square and looks disabled.
- The homeowner sees red "Missing:" boxes over the setter's shoulder.

## 8. Minor observations

- Home's "My bookings" shows only "5:35 p.m." for a Sat, Nov 14 booking, under "Tonight and upcoming". The cause is that components/schedule/meeting-card.tsx line 71 uses `formatTime` when `showLead` is set. Show the day for anything that isn't tonight.
- On the phone, the hold card wraps "p.m." alone onto the second line. Join the time and "p.m." with a non-breaking space.
- On a Sunday, Home says "Nothing left tonight" while the Schedule chip says "No meetings" for the same state; there were never any times that evening.
- Schedule carries a lot of gold: the selected chip fill, six gold-bordered tiles, the gold FAB and the gold active label. DESIGN.md's "Open: gold border" conflicts with its own Porch Light rule ("If two things on a screen are gold, one of them is wrong"), and the tile you hold loses its distinctiveness.
- Tapping a time someone else holds shows "[Name] is booking this time." in destructive red. It is information, not an error.
- The inline "Edit lead" and "Take photos" links are 44 px tall inline-flex inside a sentence, which opens a visible gap in the Map position row's line spacing.
- "They must own the home." and "They must want a price." sit next to "Edit lead", which reads as "go change the answer to Yes". Disqualifiers should look different from to-dos.
- "Which lead?" shows at most 50 leads, with no search.
- "Book this time" doesn't name the time. The commit button is the best place to repeat it ("Book Sat, Nov 14, 5:00 p.m.").
- On iPad, New lead is a stretched phone form: four empty photo placeholders of about 490 x 365 px each, and Yes / No chips about 470 px wide.
- The date strip scrolls sideways, and the only cue is a clipped chip.
- "Sign out" sits in the header of every booking screen. That is low value at the door.
- Not seen in the screenshots, judged from source only: a time held by someone else, the hold-lost alert, a failed Save and book, and the success notice on the lead page.

## 9. Questions to consider

- What if the held time never left the screen? The setter and the homeowner could keep looking at the same held tile until it is booked, instead of the app jumping to a new page that asks about leads.
- At the door, when is the answer to "Which lead?" ever not "the person in front of me"? What if the time-first path never asked it?
- What would the last screen look like if it were made to be turned around and shown to the homeowner: their address, Saturday at 5:00 p.m., who is coming, and nothing else?
