## 1. Design specificity verdict

Much of this could only be Vesper's dusk meeting. A navy screen opens on the wordmark over the homeowner's own street. Then the product's first objection ("what does it look like by day?") becomes a line the customer can drag across a house. "This house" is their own home, lit. The colour screen exists because white track once went on an off-white soffit, and the customer's initials make the colour a shared decision. The bill uses the trade's own units ("Track and lights 162 ft at $23/ft", one control box). It ends on "No payment until your install is complete." under one gold number, with no GST because GST is off. The build is also shaped by kitchens with one bar of signal: the deck opens from cache, the math and the draft live on the iPad, and a reload comes back on the same screen with the quote as typed.

Some of it is still generic scaffolding:
- **Text pages and the warranty** use a default slide template: a headline at 14vh, paragraphs, and an empty lower half, with nothing of a house or of light on them.
- **The cover** uses the placeholder text wordmark that the code says is waiting for the real logo (source).
- **The colour screen** is a stock label, field and button, not the signed card the shape asked for. After the customer signs, it still offers "Confirm again" (source).
- **Your price** should feel the most like Vesper, but two-thirds of it is stock form. Fields, toggles, add chips and steppers all use the same 1 px box. This is the "smells like software" look the shape ruled out.
- **Over photos**, two stepped translucent caption plates and a dots pill that shows as a cut-off stub on the day side look like carousel chrome, not a designed treatment.

## 3. Cognitive load

For the homeowner, the deck is light: one idea per screen, a headline and two or three lines, ten small dots, nothing else to parse. The warranty is the heaviest read (eight bullets, two different five-year terms), but it is Keenan's exact wording and right to keep.

The load spikes on Your price:
- The customer has to find the bill among about 20 identical boxes.
- The left side keeps changing under Keenan's hands.
- The gold total jumps on every keystroke, starting from $500 on a fresh quote (source).
- Lower/Upper, Per box and the add chips are choices the customer can see but can't make, so for them they are noise.

Keenan carries hidden state in his head: which screen the deck will open on, that the chevrons skip the gallery, that the colour must be set before he walks in, and that the keyboard will sit across the bill.

In Settings, a page's words and its photos live in two different cards that save differently. Words save with the Save settings button at the bottom of a long page; photos save the moment they upload.

## 4. What should change next (take 2)

1. **P0. A fresh quote opens at "Your price $500."** The starting quote is one Front run at 0 ft plus one box, so the bill shows the box alone as the price before anything is measured. Fix: show the bill lines and the total only once total feet is above 0. *(Fixed in take 1: "Adds up here as we measure.")*
2. **P0. Settling the colour means leaving through a door that opens on internal screens.** Fix: an owner-only Change on the colour screen reusing the lead page's track-colour action, and a deliberate exit. *(Fixed in take 1: the chooser opens inside the deck; the X takes two taps.)*
3. **P0. Reopening a deck resumes where the last look ended.** Fix: the entry links ask for a fresh start without changing the URL the service worker keeps. *(Fixed in take 1: #start from Tonight and the lead page; only a reload resumes.)*
4. **P1. Your price reads as Keenan's form, and the bill floats where the keyboard will cover it.** Fix: pin the bill to the top of its column, Save and its status to the builder, ticks on the toggles, one "Your price". *(Partly fixed in take 1: the bill sits high and its label is Total; the rest waits for take 2.)*
5. **P1. Photos are stored too small for this screen, and zoom drifts.** Fix: a 3200 px edge for presentation and mockup uploads; zoom around the pinch midpoint and the tapped point. *(Size fixed in take 1; zoom focus waits for take 2.)*
6. **P1. Seeded words promise more than the business can, and the colour screen talks like the app.** Fix: replacement drafts for Keenan; the colour lines in the customer's voice; never show the setter's note. *(Colour screen fixed in take 1; the seeded drafts are Keenan's to edit and are on his morning list.)*
7. **P1. Photo pages look like a generic carousel, and the gallery breaks the deck's navigation.** Fix: goBy steps through gallery photos; no pill behind the dots; one plate for title and caption; larger labels. *(Waits for take 2.)*
8. **P2. Gold is used too freely, and the text pages sit high above an empty lower half.** Fix: numerals and bullets in the muted colour; the frame lower. *(Fixed in take 1.)*
