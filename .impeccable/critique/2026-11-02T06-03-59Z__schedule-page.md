## Design specificity verdict

**Authored, not yet distinctive. The substance is Vesper's; the form could be anyone's.**

What belongs to Vesper:
- **The words and states are the evening's own.** "[Name] is booking", "Your booking", "Too late to book", the holder's initials, "Nothing left tonight. This is the next evening with an opening." and "It stays at 5:35 p.m., and new times that overlap it can't be booked." No other product would write these.
- **The gold outline on open times** is a quiet, real expression of the Porch Light idea: the times you can take are the lit ones.
- **Role shaping without a second screen.** Setters get the bare board. Keenan gets two 44 px tools, and taps on blocked and booked times that do something.

What is category-interchangeable:
- **The composition is the stock booking widget:** a strip of date chips, over a grid of slot tiles, over a list of appointment cards. Swap the copy and it is a salon booking page.
- **On the iPad it is a stretched phone column.** Each row has three 320 px tiles. The cards below are about 990 px wide, with the status about 900 px from the time. The two panes the spec allows on iPad go unused.

Missed opportunities, within the project's rules (no gradients, glass, emoji or heavy motion):
- **The board's brightness order works against the porch light.** The cream selected-evening chip and the raised booked tiles are the brightest things on screen. The open times are the darkest.
- **Clustering is hidden.** Setters book to cluster, and the cards already compute the straight-line leg between meetings. On the board, though, the neighbourhood is 12 px muted text that truncates on a phone ("TEST (automated) · TES...").
- **The day-change preview could be the board itself.** Keenan's riskiest edit would then read like the screen he already knows.

### Decision points with more than 4 visible options
- **Evening strip:** 15 evenings, about 4 visible on the phone and 10 on the iPad. They are ordered dates, so the cost is low.
- **Time grid:** 6 times here, up to 12 in Settings. That is inherent to the task, and they are in order.
- **Block time pop-up:** "The whole evening" plus every open time: 5 in the phone capture, up to 13 possible. Only one can be picked per opening.

- **Outcome form, after a meeting starts:** 4 outcomes, then 5 Not qualified reasons.
- **Pencil pop-up:** about 8 controls, ending in two saves and no way to do nothing. It is fine as a form; the final decision is the problem (issue 4).

**Setter at the door:**
- **Arrival:** calm and legible. Big, lit times and one instruction line. Confidence.
- **Peak:** the tap. The tile says "Holding" and the sheet opens. Other people's holds carry their initials, so "this time is yours" is believable. This is the best moment on the tab.
- **Valley:** a refused tap in front of the homeowner.
  - The message appears above the grid, which can be 250 px from her thumb, and pushes every tile down a line.
  - It offers no alternative, such as "Next open: 6:45 p.m."
  - A smaller valley: the homeowner's eye goes first to the raised "Your booking" tile, not to an open time.
- **End:** the sheet's done screen, which is out of scope here.

**Keenan:**
- **Arrival:** tidy, with two quiet tools that setters never see.
- **Valley 1:** he tries to close off the rest of the evening. The preselected "The whole evening" is refused with a message about a time "just taken". His trust in the tool drops on first use.
- **Valley 2, the high-stakes moment:** changing an evening that has bookings.
  - The reassurance copy is right: "It stays at 5:35 p.m."

  - There is no visible Cancel.

- **End:** the pop-up closes silently. There is no "Saturday now has its own times" line and no undo. By the peak-end rule, the riskiest task ends on its weakest beat.

## What's working

1. **The board makes the hard rule visible.**
   - It reads the database every 3 seconds.
   - A hold shows the holder's initials in a gold circle, plus "[Name] is booking" in words, so it doesn't rely on colour.
   - The tapped tile says "Holding", and every state has a plain name: "Your booking", "Too late to book", "Blocked".
   - Tiles are 80 px tall with 24 px tabular times, readable at dusk, and the gold outline marks exactly the times you can take.
2. **The day-change warning says the reassuring thing plainly.**
   - It names the meeting, the lead and the neighbourhood, and states what will not happen: "It stays at 5:35 p.m."
   - Holds that will let go are named.
   - "All days" asks first when it would touch kept bookings, holds or evenings with their own times, and lists those evenings.
   - The times recompute as the steppers move, and the past-midnight case disables saving.
   - The steppers are 44 px and also take typed numbers.
3. **One screen, shaped by role.**
   - Setters get the bare board.
   - Keenan gets two 44 px tools, and useful taps on times that can't be booked: a blocked one opens Unblock with its reason, a booked one jumps to its card.
   - The pop-up component (`DialogPanel`) is solid:
     - it uses the browser's own dialog and puts focus on the title first;
     - on the phone it is a bottom sheet, with its actions at the bottom right;
     - it leaves room for the iPhone's home bar;
     - Escape or a tap on the dimmed backdrop closes it.

## Priority issues

### 1. [P1] Block time's preselected "The whole evening" is refused on any evening with a booking, and the message says something untrue

**What:**
- "The whole evening" is the default, and it covers the span from the first time's start to the last time's end.
- `add_time_block` refuses any range that overlaps a booking. So on any evening with at least one booking (exactly the evenings Keenan most wants to close off), the default fails.
- The refusal's code, `slot_taken`, is shown with the booking sheet's words: "That time was just taken. Here are the open times now."
- On a fully booked evening, the whole evening is the only choice offered, and it always fails.
- Separately, the pop-up keeps its last choice between openings. After blocking 6:45 p.m., the next opening shows nothing selected, yet Block still acts on 6:45:
  - on the same evening, it adds a second, overlapping block, so Unblock has to be tapped twice;
  - on another evening, Block does nothing and says nothing.

**Why it matters:**
- "Close off the rest of tonight" is the first thing an owner reaches for.
- Failing on the default, with a false message, teaches Keenan that the tool can't be trusted. The only workaround is one pop-up per time.
- It also contradicts the pop-up's own hint: "A time with a booking or someone holding it can't be blocked."

**Fix:**
- Make the first choice "Every open time", with its count ("Every open time (4)"). It blocks each open time, or each run of open times, and never touches a booked or held one.
- Name the button after the choice: "Block 6:45 p.m.", "Block 4 open times".
- Give `slot_taken` its own message in this pop-up: "The 5:35 p.m. booking is in that time. Move or cancel it first, or block the open times."
- Remount the pop-up on every opening, as the pencil's pop-up already does, so the choice starts fresh.

**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden, then /impeccable clarify for the message.

### 2. [P1] The day-change preview isn't what the board will show, and by my reading of the board query, a booking can drop off the board

**What:**
- The "Times" box lists the new times: "5:00, 5:45, 6:30, 7:15, 8:00, 8:45 p.m."
- But the board leaves off any new time that overlaps a booking that starts at a different time (decisions.md, M3.5).
- The warning says "new times that overlap it can't be booked" once per booking, without naming those times, so Keenan has to work out the overlaps himself.
- Worse, I traced `schedule_board` in `20261102034109_m35_day_schedule_functions.sql` through the captured case (35-minute meetings raised to 45, with bookings at 5:00 and 5:35):
  - The 5:00 time is left off, because its new 45-minute window overlaps the kept 5:35 booking.
  - The 5:00 booking itself isn't shown in its place, because the new times still include 5:00 (the board shows a booking at its own time only when its time isn't among the new times).
  - So the 5:00 booking disappears from the board, while the warning names only the 5:35 one.
- This comes from reading the SQL, not from a capture. Confirm it with a slot check.

**Why it matters:**
- This is Keenan's riskiest edit: customers' meetings, on an evening setters are booking into.
- The preview promises six times. The board will show three or four open times plus the kept bookings.
- Setters cluster by the board, and they would no longer see the 5:00 meeting at all.

**Fix:**
- Build the preview from the same function as the board (a dry run of the proposed times), and draw it as the board in miniature, in time order:
  - kept bookings where they are;
  - the new open times;
  - left-off times struck through, with the reason ("overlaps the 5:35 p.m. booking").
- Name the left-off times in the warning.
- Keep a one-line summary above the buttons, such as "2 bookings stay; 5:45 and 6:30 won't be offered", so it is visible whenever a save is possible.
- Fix the board so every live booking always shows.
- Add a slot check for a longer meeting length where a lined-up booking is followed by a kept one.
- This touches slots, so the race tests and slot checks in CLAUDE.md apply.

**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden for the board and the stale preview, /impeccable clarify for the preview.

### 3. [P2] The board's brightness runs backwards: taken times and the date outshine the open times

**What:**
- Open tiles are dark, with a thin gold outline.
- Booked tiles have the raised secondary fill and full-strength white text.
- The selected evening chip is a solid cream block, the brightest object on the screen.
- The spec says booked times are "greyed out", and DESIGN.md says booked, blocked and closed times are "muted".
- Meanwhile, the booked tile's one line that helps clustering, the neighbourhood, is 12 px muted text that truncates on a phone ("TEST (automated) · TES...").
- The evening on screen ("Tonight", "Saturday, November 14") is a 14 px label under a 24 px "Schedule", which the tab bar already says.

**Why it matters:**
- At the door, the homeowner is looking at the setter's phone. The first filled tile they see is "Your booking", which reads as the time on offer.
- A setter scanning for an opening in bright sun has to read labels instead of seeing which tiles are lit.
- It breaks the Porch Light rule: the brightest thing on screen should be the next action.

**Fix:**
- **Booked tiles go flat:** page background and a quiet border, with the time in muted text.
  - Promote the neighbourhood to 14 px foreground on its own line. The setter's name can truncate; the neighbourhood shouldn't.
  - For Keenan, add a "tap to see" cue, the way blocked tiles say "tap to unblock".
- **Blocked and closed times** stay muted.
- **The selected chip** becomes quieter but still unmistakable: secondary fill, a 2 px foreground border and bold text, instead of a solid cream block.
- **The evening name** ("Tonight", or the date) becomes the board's heading, at title size.

**Suggested command:** /impeccable quieter, then /impeccable typeset.

### 4. [P2] The pencil pop-up can't do nothing

**What:**
- Its only actions are "All days" and "Just this day". There is no Cancel, and both buttons stay live with nothing changed.
- The database always writes a day schedule for "Just this day". So tapping the gold button just to get out gives the evening its own times, and it quietly stops following future "All days" changes.
- When there is no conflict, "All days" rewrites Settings for every usual evening that doesn't have its own times, in one tap. What it reaches is explained only on the confirmation screen.
- The preview has no "checking" state:
  - a quick tap of "All days" before the preview returns can skip that confirmation;
  - a failed preview removes or freezes the warning, with no message.
- On the phone capture, one warning already brings the sheet up to the header. With two warnings, as in the iPad capture, the second scrolls below the fold while both saves stay live.

**Why it matters:**
- This is the one pop-up that can change every evening, and it is the one without an exit.
- Keenan discovers the side effect days later, when "All days" lists "These evenings keep their own times: Sat, Nov 14".

**Fix:**
- Add Cancel on the left.
- Keep both saves disabled until a value differs from the evening's current times, and while the preview is checking ("Checking bookings...").
- Put one line above the buttons saying what each does, for example "All days: every Mon to Sat evening without its own times, and Settings".
- Show the evening's current state at the top: "Uses the usual times" or "Has its own times".
- After saving, confirm in one line on the board, with Undo for a day change.

**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden, then /impeccable clarify.

### 5. [P2] On the iPad, Keenan's evening sits below the board, and the two follow the strip at different speeds

**What:**
- In landscape, the page is a stretched phone column:
  - three 320 px tiles a row;
  - then full-width cards, about 990 px wide, with the content hugging the left and the status about 900 px from the time.
- Tapping a booked tile scrolls the page down to its card, and the board out of view.
- Separately, on any device, the strip switches evenings at once, but the bookings list and the two header tools only catch up after a round trip to the server.
- There is no pending cue while that happens. On weak signal, the board can show Friday while the list, and Block time, still act on Saturday. The pop-up's title is the only safeguard.

**Why it matters:**
- The iPad is one of Keenan's two devices for this tab, and the spec allows two panes there.
- Scrolling between the board and the cards is friction he will repeat on every evening he plans.

**Fix:**
- From about 1024 px wide, use two panes:
  - the strip and the board on the left, with tiles two to a row;
  - the evening's bookings on the right, scrolling on their own.
- A tap on a booked tile highlights its card and scrolls the right pane only.
- Drive the tools and the list from the same selected day as the board.
- While the server catches up, dim the list and show "Loading Saturday's bookings".

**Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt, then /impeccable harden for the lag.

## Minor observations

- **"p.m." on every tile** is set at full size and weight. Set it smaller and lighter so the digits carry. Keep it, though: a day schedule can start before noon.
- **Times in one row don't line up.** Tile content is centred vertically, so a tile with a third line pushes its time up (iPad: 5:00 against 5:35). Align tile content to the top.
- **The evening is named three times** on the owner's screen: the chip, the label, and "Bookings, Saturday, November 14".
- **The native time field** reads "05:00 PM", while the rest of the app reads "5:00 p.m.".
- **Two golds in one place.** The warning box has a gold border, directly above the gold "Just this day". Use a foreground border and a bold lead-in instead. Red stays for errors only.
- **No tick on Block time's chosen answer**, unlike AnswerGroup's chosen answers.
- **Booked tiles give Keenan no cue** that a tap opens the card. Blocked tiles say "tap to unblock".
- **"Block time" dead-ends on an evening with no times.** It stays live there, then says "This evening has no meeting times to block." Hide it on those evenings, or point to the pencil.
- **"No meeting times this evening."** could tell Keenan he can open the evening with the pencil, since a day schedule can open an evening that isn't a usual meeting day. The setter's version is fine as it is.
- **The Sunday note** (from the code, not captured) reads "Nothing left tonight", though there were never any times. "No meetings tonight" fits better.
- **"0.0 km in a straight line from the 5:00 p.m. meeting"** comes from test coordinates here. It will also appear for real neighbours, which is exactly what clustering aims for. "Under 100 m from the 5:00 p.m. meeting" reads better.
- **Card photos show only for tonight**, so when planning tomorrow, Keenan can't glance at the rooflines.
- **Block time's list of times can be a little stale.** It comes from the last server render, not the live board, so a time held seconds ago is still offered. The database refuses it with the holder's name, which is fine.
- **Light theme** (computed, not captured): gold on white is about 2.9:1, under the 3:1 guideline for a control's outline. The word "Open" carries the state, but setters knock in bright sun. Consider a 2 px border in the light theme.
- **Screen readers aren't told about changes** from the 3-second board reads. A short announcement when the focused time is held or booked would help.
- **DESIGN.md is behind the build.** It says the selected evening and chosen answers are gold and booked times are muted. The build uses a light fill for selection and a raised fill for booked times. Decide which is right, then document it (/impeccable document).
