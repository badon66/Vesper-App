# Shape: the Session tab

1. **Job and audience.** A setter on the street from about 4:00 pm, phone in one hand, between doors and sometimes with the homeowner beside them. Keenan knocks too and gets the same tab. Mode: Operate. The tab is where the evening runs from: start, count, book, and end.
2. **Outcome and proof.** Every door and conversation counted with one thumb, a booking started from tonight's open times without leaving the tab, and back on the tab after every save ("Lead saved. On to the next door."). The end summary (time out, doors, conversations, leads, bookings) is the proof the session happened.
3. **Direction.** The app's existing world (DESIGN.md), denser than Home. No new visual ideas: the neighbourhood is the page's heading while knocking, the counters stay the gold buttons people already know but shorter, the open times reuse Schedule's time buttons exactly, and everything else is quiet outline buttons. Gold stays for the counters and the times (the two things tapped all evening); New lead and End knocking are outline buttons.
4. **Scope and boundaries.** The Session route with its two states, the tab bar change (Session takes New lead's place and look), the knock card removed from both Homes (Branch B adds a Start knocking button or a live-session bar there), Unfinished bookings moved from Home, New lead's save landing on Session while a session runs, and a New lead button at the top of Leads. Schedule stays as it is. Anti-goals: anything that scrolls at 430 x 932 while knocking with no drafts; a second, different time picker; red for anything that isn't an error; animation beyond what the app already does.
5. **States and ranges.** Not knocking: first ever (no neighbourhood yet), with a last neighbourhood, with recent ones (up to four); nobody else out, several out, some finished for the day. Knocking: just started, hours in, start fixed earlier, taps saving, retrying, a session someone ended elsewhere; tonight with open times, tonight full (next evening's times), no times at all; no drafts, one, several. Ending: confirm, summary, back to start. View as: everything acted out on screen, nothing saved.
6. **Interaction and layout.** Not knocking, top to bottom: the summary of the session just ended (when there is one), Neighbourhood with recent ones as one-tap chips under it, Start knocking (gold, full width), then Unfinished when there are drafts, then Out today (name, neighbourhood, since when, doors, conversations; people still out first). Knocking, top to bottom: the neighbourhood as the heading with "Started 4:32 pm. Out 1 h 12 min." and a Fix button that opens a time field in place; the two counters side by side (about 96 px tall, Undo under each); the saving line; tonight's open times with an "All evenings" link beside the heading; New lead (outline, smaller); Unfinished when there are drafts; End knocking, which asks first and then shows the summary.
7. **Constraints.** Fixing the start time: any time earlier today, never in the future, only while the session runs; checked on the server. Taps keep their queue and retry. One open session per person stays a database rule. View as reads the viewed person's sessions and acts out every write. Plain copy, no em dashes. Assumptions: Unfinished also shows while not knocking, so a draft is never out of sight; "Out today" includes people who already finished, after the ones still out; the end summary's bookings are this session's leads that have a booking still on.
