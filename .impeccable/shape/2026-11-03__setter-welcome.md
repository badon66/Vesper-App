# Shape: a setter's first sign-in and their notifications

1. **Job and audience.** A new setter, or Zane who already uses the app, on their own phone, usually in Safari right after Keenan texted them a password, often at home before a first evening. Mode: Operate (get the phone ready, three device settings), then Read (one screen on how a door goes). Second audience: Keenan, who resets a setter's welcome from People and checks it with View as.
2. **Outcome and proof.** The phone is ready for the door: Vesper on the home screen (it opens like an app, and on iPhone that is what lets it take notifications), location allowed (so "Use where I'm standing" works on a street Google doesn't have yet), notifications on (so they hear when Keenan marks, moves or cancels one of their bookings). Then they know the flow: a knock session on Home, a time on Schedule, the booking sheet's three steps. Each step shows what this phone actually says, read from the device, never assumed.
3. **Direction.** The booking sheet's world, inside the app's frame. One step per screen with a slim four-part progress bar at the top (Home screen, Location, Notifications, How a door goes); a title, one sentence on why, the one action, and a status line that says what the phone answered. A bottom bar that is always there, in thumb reach: Back on the left, Next on the right (primary), with Skip this step and Skip the welcome as the quiet actions above it. The iPhone steps are a short numbered list naming the Share button and "Add to Home Screen" in words. The tour is a numbered list of three, then Start knocking, which ends the welcome and goes Home. Assumption: the header stays (wordmark, bell, theme, sign out, and View as's gold bar), and the tab bar steps aside here as it does on the lead form.
4. **Scope and boundaries.** /app/welcome for setters only (Keenan and crew go Home); /app/notifications for setters (Keenan's card stays in Settings, and his /app/notifications goes there); the bell in the header for setters; People shows each setter's welcome and Reset welcome. The booking sheet, Schedule and Keenan's Settings card don't change. Anti-goals: confetti or celebration, a carousel, marketing copy, emoji, gradients, forcing a permission, blocking the app, a tour that points at every button.
5. **States and ranges.** Home screen: already in the app, iPhone Safari, iPhone in another browser, Android, a computer. Location: not asked yet, allowed, blocked, no location on this device, no answer in time. Notifications: keys not set yet in Vercel (true today), iPhone not on the home screen yet, this browser can't, allowed and on, blocked, didn't save, a device another account used. View as: every control disabled, the setter's own devices listed, nothing written. Returning: a setter who left halfway sees the welcome again from Home next time; once finished or skipped, never again unless Keenan resets it or they open it from Notifications.
6. **Interaction and layout.** One column, 16 px gutters, 44 px targets, the action in the middle of the screen; the step title takes focus when the step changes and the progress reads "Step 2 of 4"; the status line is a polite live region; a step's action says "Asking" while the phone's prompt is up, and the status keeps the answer. Finishing says "One moment" while it saves and stays put with a plain line if it can't.
7. **Constraints.** Nothing is written while rendering; finishing and every device action refuse View as on the server; the welcome never shows to Keenan or crew; plain copy, no em dashes; notices on a lock screen name the street only.
