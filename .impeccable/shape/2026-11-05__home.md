# Shape: the new Homes

1. **Job and audience.** Setters open Home before and after knocking, phone in hand, to see the team's evening (where Keenan is going, which areas are booked, so they cluster) and how their own bookings went. Keenan opens Home on his phone through the day and the evening to see today's meetings and what each still needs, and who's out. Mode: Operate, read at a glance.
2. **Outcome and proof.** A setter answers "what's on tonight, and how did mine go?" without a tap; Keenan answers "what are my meetings, what's missing for each, and who's out?" without a tap. Start knocking (or the live session bar) is one tap from either Home.
3. **Direction.** The existing world (DESIGN.md), list-first and quiet. One switch, Today and Tomorrow, two plain segments, no gold on the chosen one (gold stays for primary actions). The live session bar is the only gold-bordered element on a setter's Home: it is the evening's way back into Session. Meetings are rows, not cards, for setters (time, area, who booked it); Keenan keeps the meeting cards he marks outcomes on, with the Meetings page's flags added.
4. **Scope and boundaries.** Both Homes. Setters: Start knocking or the live-session bar; the team's schedule (today, switch to tomorrow: every meeting, its time, area and who booked it, never another setter's customer's phone or email); How mine went (their own bookings and how each meeting went); Follow-ups due as now. Keenan: Start knocking or the bar; today's meetings with the switch to tomorrow, each with the Meetings page's flags (no mockup, no track colour, N of 8 collected, the outcome once marked); who's out knocking now. Untouched: Session, Schedule, the Meetings page, outcomes and pay rules. Anti-goals: the open times grid on Home (it lives on Session and Schedule now); customer names or contact details of other setters' leads; numbers without saying what they count.
5. **States and ranges.** No meetings today, several, a full evening (6 to 8); tomorrow empty; meetings moved or cancelled (muted or left out); a setter with no bookings yet, with some marked, with a Not qualified reason; nobody out knocking, several; Keenan knocking himself; a session left open from yesterday (closes on Session). View as: everything as the person sees it, nothing written.
6. **Interaction and layout.** Setter, top to bottom: greeting; Start knocking (gold, full width) or the live bar ("Knocking in Bridgeland. Out 1 h 12 min. 24 doors." with a chevron, opens Session); Team schedule with the Today and Tomorrow switch; How mine went; Follow-ups due. Keenan, top to bottom: Today and the date; Start knocking or the bar; Meetings with the switch; Out knocking now. The switch changes the list in place (no reload), remembers nothing, and opens on Today.
7. **Constraints.** Every query scoped to the person the screen is for (View as reads with Keenan's session). Setters read every booking (team rule) but the team schedule asks for no contact columns at all. Plain copy, no em dashes. Assumptions: "How mine went" covers the last 14 days and anything upcoming, newest evening first for the past and soonest first for what's coming; Keenan's Home drops the team-today totals and today's leads (not in his list; M6 brings the dashboard).
