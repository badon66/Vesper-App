# Shape: booking sheet round 3 (2026-11-05)

1. **Who and when.** A setter at a stranger's door at dusk, phone in one hand, the homeowner warming up; then on the sidewalk finishing details. Keenan on his iPhone 15 Pro Max (430 x 932) and setters on 390-wide iPhones, installed as home-screen apps.
2. **What went wrong in the field (Keenan, after a day of using it).** Answers come out of order, and the sheet stopped them at every step with a question open. People don't give a number to someone who just knocked, so asking for it first cost bookings. "Use where I'm standing" seemed to do nothing.
3. **Mode.** Operate. Speed, one thumb, and never being stopped matter more than anything decorative.
4. **Scope.** The booking sheet's flow (Next never blocks; markers; the counter; Book as the only gate with its missing list), the phone moved to Book with Call or text beside it, the location button and the address field it fills, and four optional one-tap answers (house type, setter's read, hot buttons, call or text) on the sheet, New lead, the lead page and Keenan's Meetings and Home cards. Untouched: holds, the countdown, the time menu, Before you book's rules, meeting mode, anything a customer sees.
5. **Success.** A setter can take the answers in the order the conversation gives them, ask for the number last, and Book tells them exactly what is still missing with one tap to each. The location button visibly works, or says exactly why not.
6. **Interaction and layout.**
   - Next is always "Next" (or "Return to Book" when a step was opened from Book's list); swiping always moves.
   - The progress bar keeps its six segments in three parts; a step passed with something missing gets a small dot under its segment, in Night Text, not gold or red. The counter reads "Step 3 of 6, 7 to answer" (answers, the same count as Book's button).
   - Book: the summary; "Still to answer (N)" as rows with the step's name and a chevron, each a 44 px tap; or "Everything is answered."; the ruled-out note with Keep as a lead; the distance warning; then, last and pinned to the bottom of the screen above the Book button, the phone with Not collected and Call / Text. The Book button names the time when ready; until then it reads "N left to answer" in the quiet dashed style.
   - The learned step's optional answers sit under a hairline after the four counted questions: Setter's read as one row of three, the hot buttons as a two-column grid of checks, Not sure exclusive.
   - The house step's house type is one row of four after the soffit.
   - Location: a spinner and "Finding where you are..." the moment it's tapped, the best reading so far ("about 120 m so far, getting closer"), then "Position saved from this phone..., accurate to about 8 m." and the nearest address filled into an empty box for checking, or offered with "Use it" when something is typed. Failures are a calm bordered note (never red) with numbered iPhone steps.
7. **Constraints.** Navy and gold, dark by default, no gradients, glass or emoji; chosen answers light on dark with a tick, gold only for the next action; 44 px targets; no em dashes; no screen scrolls at 430 or 390 where it can be avoided (the learned step, holding six groups at Keenan's request, may scroll a little at 390).
8. **Open question, answered by the build.** Saving: a lead still saves only with every question answered, so the first save is usually Book's; photos before it upload to the setter's own folder and attach at the save.
